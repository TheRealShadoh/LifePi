LifePi privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026

LifePi is a free, open-source life counter for Magic: The Gathering (source code). This policy covers the LifePi app for Android from Google Play, the web version at therealshadoh.github.io/LifePi, and the Raspberry Pi version.

The short version

LifePi has no accounts and no analytics of its own, and we don't sell your data. Your games stay on your device. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob on its menu screens, unless you buy Remove ads. Google collects some device data to show those ads, as described below.

What stays on your device

Life totals, players, card choices, history and settings are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. They are not sent to us.

Card search and card art (Scryfall)

When you search for a card, or LifePi picks random card art, the app asks Scryfall for card details and images. Scryfall receives what you typed in the search, plus the technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address. See Scryfall's privacy policy.

Ads (Android app)

The Android app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, only while the menu, history or dice screen is open. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

DataExamples
Device and advertising identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID
IP addresscan be used to estimate your general location
App interactionsapp launches, ad views and taps
Diagnosticsapp and ad performance

This data is encrypted in transit. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks before personalised ads are shown, and Menu > Ad privacy lets you change your choice at any time. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads), or remove ads entirely with the one-time Remove ads purchase. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The optional Remove ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment details, only confirmation that the purchase was made, which the app uses to turn ads off on your devices.

Phones & TV (Raspberry Pi version)

The Pi version can show the game to phones and TVs on your own Wi-Fi network. That traffic stays on your network and is protected by a PIN.

Children

LifePi is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. For questions, open an issue at github.com/TheRealShadoh/LifePi/issues.
